Frequently Asked Questions

How does NDVI satellite tracking work?

We pull multispectral data from Sentinel-2 satellites over your farm's coordinate sector. By assessing near-infrared light reflectance, we estimate leaf density and chlorophyll indicators, warning you if values fall.

Is my financial data stored securely offline?

Yes. All offline ledger entries are held locally in your browser's encrypted LocalStorage index. Once network sync is active, records are pushed to security-hardened cloud clusters.

How do I verify my digital farmer ID passport?

Navigate to your profile settings, select KYC Verification, and drag-and-drop your land record extraction certificate. Our administrative coordinators verify credentials within 24 hours.
