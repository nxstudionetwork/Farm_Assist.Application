Frequently Asked Questions

How do I contact support?

You can reach our support team through the Help & Support page, by emailing support@farmassist.in, or by calling our helpline at 1800-180-1551 (Toll Free). Our team is available Monday to Saturday, 8 AM to 8 PM IST.

Where can I find tutorials and guides?

Head over to the Learning Hub from the main menu. We offer step-by-step video tutorials, written guides, and interactive walkthroughs covering everything from setting up your farm profile to interpreting NDVI satellite maps.

How do I report a bug?

Use the feedback form above and select "Report a Problem" as the type. Include as much detail as possible: what you were doing, what you expected, and what actually happened. Our developers review every report within 48 hours.
